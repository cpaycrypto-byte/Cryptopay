Our Origin Story

Why Traditional Banking Failed the Digital World

For decades, international commerce and cross-border remittances were held hostage by legacy correspondent banking chains. Slow clearing windows, opaque wire fees, and 3-to-5 day settlement delays made financial agility impossible.

CryptoPay was created by seasoned Wall Street institutional technologists and distributed cryptography researchers to build the single portal where fiat banking and cryptographic assets coexist seamlessly. Today, our infrastructure powers personal wealth preservation, global contractor payroll, and institutional liquidity around the clock.
