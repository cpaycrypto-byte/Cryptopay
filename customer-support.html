Frequently Asked Questions

How long do cryptocurrency deposits take to credit?

Cryptocurrency deposits are credited as soon as minimum on-chain block confirmations are reached (2 for Bitcoin, 12 for Ethereum, 3 for USDT TRC20/ERC20, and instant ~400ms for Solana). Internal transfers between CryptoPay users settle in 0.00 seconds.

Are my cryptocurrency holdings insured?

Yes. Digital assets stored in CryptoPay institutional multi-signature cold vaults are backed by comprehensive crime insurance covering physical loss, theft, and cybersecurity breaches. Fiat USD reserves are held in segregated partner custodial accounts.

What is the 9-digit authorization code used for?

The 9-digit security code acts as an end-to-end cryptographic transaction authorization key (assigned to you upon account approval). It safeguards internal transfers, bill payments, and on-chain withdrawals against unauthorized access.

How do CryptoPay Staking Vault yields work?

CryptoPay automatically stakes deposited assets in verified proof-of-stake validator nodes and institutional liquidity pools. Earned yields (up to 12.5% APY) are calculated continuously and credited daily directly to your account.
