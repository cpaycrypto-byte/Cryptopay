2. Global Privacy Policy (GDPR / CCPA)

Data Sovereignty, Biometric Storage & Confidentiality Protections
GDPR & CCPA Compliant

CryptoPay is committed to safeguarding personal data in accordance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and global financial secrecy standards.

2.1 Information We Collect

  • Identity Verification Data: Legal full name, date of birth, government-issued passport/ID number, biometric facial verification scans, and proof of residence.
  • Financial Information: Linked bank routing/account numbers, wallet addresses, transaction hashes, and source of funds documentation.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP addresses, browser fingerprint, operating system details, access timestamps, and geolocation coordinates.

2.2 How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized strictly to provide digital banking operations, prevent financial crime, satisfy statutory AML/KYC obligations, and secure your account against unauthorized intrusion. We never sell, monetize, or broker customer personal data to third-party advertisers.

2.3 Data Encryption & Vault Safeguards

All customer records are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM cryptography and transmitted over TLS 1.3 tunnels. Biometric identity vectors are isolated in dedicated hardware security modules (HSMs) and cannot be decrypted by standard platform operators.

2.4 Data Retention & Erasure Rights

Under international anti-money laundering regulations, certain financial and identification records must be retained for a mandatory statutory period (typically 5 to 7 years following account closure). Subject to these statutory laws, users retain the right to request access, correction, or data portability.

3. Digital Asset & Crypto Risk Disclosure

Mandatory Consumer Protection & Market Volatility Notification
Risk Disclosure
High-Risk Warning: The prices of digital assets are subject to extreme market volatility. The value of your holdings may experience rapid appreciation or decline. Never allocate funds you cannot afford to lose. Cryptocurrency assets held in custody are not insured by the FDIC or SIPC.

3.1 Price Volatility & Market Liquidity

Cryptocurrency prices can fluctuate substantially over short time horizons. Market movements are influenced by global macroeconomics, speculative sentiment, protocol governance upgrades, and regulatory policy shifts. CryptoPay does not guarantee exchange execution prices during periods of extreme market illiquidity.

3.2 Irreversibility of Blockchain Transactions

Transactions broadcasted to decentralized blockchains (including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and USDT TRC20/ERC20) are computationally final and irreversible. If you transfer digital assets to an incorrect destination address or utilize an incompatible network standard, neither CryptoPay nor any central entity can recall, reverse, or refund those assets.

3.3 Smart Contract & Staking Protocol Risks

Yield and staking vaults interact with cryptographic smart contracts and decentralized proof-of-stake validation protocols. While all protocol contracts undergo third-party smart contract security audits, zero-day vulnerabilities, validator slashing events, or hard forks inherently carry operational risk.

3.4 Regulatory & Legislative Uncertainty

The legal and regulatory framework governing digital assets is continuously evolving worldwide. Future legislative acts, executive orders, or regulatory sanctions may restrict or prohibit the purchase, custody, transfer, or yield generation of digital assets in particular regions.

4. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & KYC Policy

CryptoPay enforces strict adherence to the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) Recommendations, the Bank Secrecy Act (BSA), and EU Anti-Money Laundering Directives (AMLD6).

4.1 Multi-Tier Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

  • Tier 1: Verification of email, phone number, and basic demographic data. Limited to small daily transfer volumes.
  • Tier 2 (Full KYC): Mandatory submission of government ID (Passport, National ID, Driver's License) with 3D liveness biometric validation and utility bill verification.
  • Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD): Applied to Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and accounts exceeding $100,000 in monthly throughput, requiring verified Source of Wealth documentation.

4.2 Continuous On-Chain Chainalysis Monitoring

All cryptocurrency deposits and withdrawals undergo real-time heuristic analysis against sanctioned wallets (OFAC, UN, EU), mixing services (e.g., Tornado Cash), and ransomware addresses. High-risk deposits are automatically quarantined pending compliance review.

5. Customer Complaints & Dispute Policy

We are dedicated to providing fair, equitable, and prompt resolution of any customer dissatisfaction or transactional disputes.

5.1 How to File an Official Complaint

If your issue is not resolved through our standard 24/7 Support Desk, you may file a formal complaint by emailing complaints@cryptopay.global with your Account Number, Transaction Reference, and detailed description.

5.2 Resolution Timelines

  • Acknowledgment: Within 24 hours of ticket receipt.
  • Investigation & Preliminary Findings: Within 3 business days.
  • Final Written Decision: Within 15 business days for standard matters (extended to 35 days in exceptionally complex cross-border banking matters).

6. Cookie & Tracking Technologies Policy

CryptoPay uses cookies and similar cryptographic session tokens to authenticate user requests, preserve user interface preferences, and protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF).

6.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are technically required to authenticate your login session, encrypt communications, and prevent session hijacking. They cannot be disabled.

6.2 Analytical Cookies

Anonymized metrics are collected to understand page response latency, error rates, and improve server routing. We do not correlate analytical telemetry with your individual financial balances.
